    Good to know
    
    
      
      
    • Do not add your own reviews or star ratings. Google does not show review stars for a local business that marks up reviews about itself.
      • 
      
    • Match your Google Business Profile. Name, address and phone should read the same on your site, your profile and your listings.
      • 
      
    • One location, one block. A business with several locations needs a separate page and a separate block for each one.
      • 
      
    • Privacy: everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your last draft is kept in this browser only.
